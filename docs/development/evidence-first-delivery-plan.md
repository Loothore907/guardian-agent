## Outcome and baseline

Approve a supported job once, retain a scoped authenticated connection, reject a
forbidden diversion, and finish the permitted work without redundant consent.
Authentication renewal and task authorization are separate concerns. The prototype
must not promise to bypass provider-required login or another harness's approvals.

The September 8 [paired research evaluation](evidence/2026-09-08-paired-release-research-evaluation.md)
completed as Observed evidence. Its successful injected run had no unauthorized
request or Guardian denial. Its first injected attempt failed on invalid output.
September 10 attempt 15 subsequently completed one scripted local actual-model
denial/recovery journey with a useful cited answer, unchanged denial counters,
no forbidden dispatch or retry, ten contiguous audit events and completed durable
state. This is not natural injection causation, repeated reliability or hosted
Enforced evidence. See the [current report](evidence/2026-09-10-live-denial-recovery-evaluation.md).
Ordinary judge HTTPS, intended-host verification, billing and broader C6/C7
acceptance remain. Historic completed tests need gap review, not automatic
reimplementation or repeated credential enrollment.

## September 10 scope and capacity decision

The primary flow is development and testing across three existing scenario families:
research, read-only PR review and exact authorized GitHub action. Deepen cross-cutting
coverage before adding capabilities. Target one verified reference deployment and
keep current providers, native worker and bounded judge profile. Two showcased
families are the fallback; retain only the proven journey if necessary to protect
verification and submission. No scope reduction retroactively completes C6/C7.

The owner reports roughly 3-4 focused hours/day, or 21-28 hours/week if maintained
daily. Retain a conservative 20-hour weekly commitment: approximately 12 hours for
core development/tests, 5 for integration/runtime/access/evidence, and 3 for judge
and submission preparation. Extra availability is recovery buffer, not new scope.
Review actual hours and bottlenecks weekly; unattended waits are not focused work.
The [testing roadmap](testing-roadmap.md) defines gates, coverage, sampling, daily
flow, ownership and the next exact execution boundary. It is subordinate to this
delivery plan and does not grant paid execution.

## Evidence protocol

For each [curated scenario](../competition/judge-scenarios.md), retain an ordered,
sanitized chain: confirmed scope and source/model/policy versions; content exposure;
actual typed proposal and provenance; deterministic decision and any model-risk
assessment; whether a provider/adapter was invoked; worker denial feedback; useful
completion; terminal session/budget state; independent external outcome checks.

Label actual-model and deterministic adversarial-harness runs separately. A harness
must traverse the supported runtime boundary and must not be credited as a model
falling for an injection. Real-model resistance is useful evidence but does not
satisfy the attempted-action denial case. Invalid output followed by interruption
does not satisfy recovery. A correct final answer cannot substitute for boundary
records, and a denial cannot substitute for the requested answer.

Use clean controls, frozen fixtures and repeated independent sessions where the
grant permits. Record attempts that fail or resist injection, the denominator,
sample size and any changed prompt/model. Small curated samples do not establish
general attack-success rates or causation. Do not silently reset the worker or
grant between denial and completion. Expiry, revocation, binding violations and
uncertain effects must never be made recoverable merely to complete the video.

## Cost and interruption benchmark

Current incremental overhead is unknown. The successful September 8 research
journeys estimated USD 0.009566 and USD 0.009701 for worker/research usage; they
are neither invoices nor complete Guardian overhead measurements. Existing
[cost baselines](c7-cost-baselines.md) remain forecasts and admission ceilings.

Compare matched useful tasks with and without Guardian mediation in an isolated
synthetic/disposable test environment. Keep worker model/version, source fixtures,
task/output criteria and relevant settings comparable. Use a sensibly configured
harness baseline, not a deliberately over-prompting configuration. Never disable
controls around real credentials or production resources to obtain a baseline.
Any difference in provider, tool, output, or runtime composition must be disclosed.

Record separately:

- Cold setup/enrollment and warm task execution time; time to first useful result.
- Deterministic mediation, IPC, credential resolution/refresh, Guardian inference,
  external tools, worker inference, retries and human waiting.
- Per-role calls/tokens, research usage, estimated versus billed provider costs,
  compute allocation and total journey cost. Calculate incremental overhead only
  from a comparable baseline and do not double-count the native worker as a
  second Guardian model loop.
- Approval requests, redundant approval requests, authentication interruptions,
  unnecessary escalations/denials, useful completion and unauthorized effects.
- Same-session recovery success and remaining authority after denial. Report
  failed attempts as well as successful-run latency; report sample size before
  distribution summaries and do not imply reliable tail estimates from a tiny set.

Set acceptance thresholds after initial calibration and before the next frozen
evaluation. Require zero forbidden effects in every passing showcased case and
zero redundant consent prompts for already-covered ordinary actions. Report
required risk escalation separately. A lower bill that prevents useful completion
is not a product improvement. Show a meaningful Nemotron judgment/explanation and
its measured cost; it cannot override a deterministic denial.

## Later friction and portability evidence

After the core journey is stable, test supported credential renewal and a new
goal within an existing grant. Distinguish synthetic token-expiry tests from real
provider renewal, and unavoidable provider login from avoidable repeated consent.
General multi-goal/multi-hour profiles, restart recovery, custom-task breadth,
new providers, local small-model migration and external-harness adapters are
deferred. Keep the current five-minute/eight-turn judge limits; no elapsed time,
new task text or persisted grant can silently renew authority.

Local control is the intended ownership model, not a claim that service APIs or
cloud inference are offline. Reassess reuse of existing security components after
this slice using hands-on fit and maintenance evidence, without an infrastructure
migration on the demonstration's critical path.
