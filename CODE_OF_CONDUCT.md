# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and harassment-free project community. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give security reports appropriate confidentiality and care.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, licenses, and responsible disclosure.
- Avoid overstating security guarantees or another contributor's work.
- Help contributors understand both the problem and the safe path forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, credential exposure, malicious exploitation, and retaliation against good-faith reporters are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The maintainer may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community and project.
